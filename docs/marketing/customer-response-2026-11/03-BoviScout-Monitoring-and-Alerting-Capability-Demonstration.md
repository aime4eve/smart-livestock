> ⚠️ **DATA PROVENANCE NOTICE — READ FIRST**

## 1. System Data Flow

| Layer | Content | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Device telemetry (base)** | Rumen bolus (temperature / motility / activity) and tracker (GPS / steps / distance) raw telemetry | ~1 frame every 30 minutes |
| **Behavior monitoring** | Temperature curve, motility rate, activity grading, estrus score (per frame); drinking behavior (nightly batch) | Per frame (~30 min); drinking next morning |
| **AI health observation** | Models the last 24 h of telemetry into a 0–1 anomaly index (calm / watch / alarm) | Every 5 minutes |
| **Alert response** | Health alerts (temperature / motility / estrus / AI) and fence & location alerts | Immediate on threshold breach; epidemic scan hourly |
| **Epidemic aggregation** | One farm-wide warning when ≥10% of the herd had health alerts in the last 7 days; auto-resolves below 5% | Checked hourly |

Device alerts (low battery / offline / weak signal / tamper) form a data-link assurance layer operating across all telemetry.

## 3. Health Alert Rules (as configured in the platform)

| Alert | Trigger (summary) | Severity | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abnormal Body Temperature | Individual baseline maintained per animal; alert at ≥ +1.0 °C; fever at ≥ +1.5 °C; persistent fever if ≥ +1.0 °C for 2+ h; severe if ≥ +2.0 °C or ≥ 41.0 °C absolute | Warning / Critical | Auto-resolves on return to normal; hourly reconciliation |
| Abnormal Rumen Motility | Motility below the lower of 50% of individual baseline and the clinical floor of 1.0/min (default baseline 2.0/min) | Warning | Auto-resolves on recovery |
| Estrus Detection | Composite score ≥ 70/100 (steps 40% + temperature deviation 30% + distance 30%); breeding window ~12 h | Warning | Auto-resolves below 70 |
| Herd Epidemic Warning | ≥10% of herd with health alerts in last 7 days; one farm-wide alert; hourly scan | Critical | Auto-resolves below 5% |
| AI Health Anomaly | AI anomaly score ≥ 0.7 (Critical ≥ 0.85) from 24-h multi-source modeling; 60-min per-animal dedup | Warning / Critical | Auto-resolves below 0.5 |

## 4. Device & Location Alert Rules

| Alert | Trigger (summary) |
|---|---|
| Device Tamper | Built-in tamper sensor reports abnormal status — immediate, manual handling required |
| Device Low Battery | Battery < 20% |
| Device Offline | No data for > 2 h (checked every 10 min) |
| Weak Device Signal | 30-day average RSSI < −100 dBm (watch band −90 to −100 dBm) |
| Fence Breach | GPS outside fence boundary beyond buffer (default 50 m) — Critical |
| Fence Approach | Outside boundary but within buffer |
| Livestock Outlier | Distance to nearest gateway > herd baseline (median + 3×MAD) and > 1,500 m |
| Not-Returned Roll-call | > 200 m from farm base at roll-call check |

## 5. Monitoring Views Demonstrated

- **Temperature curve:** 72-hour per-animal curve at 5-minute resolution against the individual baseline (default 38.5 °C), with fever markers and daily fever duration.
- **Rumen motility:** current rate (displayed as "rumination frequency"), 24-hour trend vs individual baseline, intensity heatmap.
- **Drinking behavior:** daily drinking count and time-of-day distribution from V-shaped temperature-dip detection; fever windows automatically excluded. *(Drinking events only — no water-volume estimate.)*
- **Estrus score:** 0–100 composite score per animal; ≥ 70 suggests a breeding window.
- **AI health observation:** anomaly index bands — calm (<0.3) / watch (0.3–0.7) / alarm (≥0.7) — re-evaluated every 5 minutes; alarm band escalates to the AI Health Anomaly alert.

## 6. What This Document Demonstrates — and What It Does Not

This document demonstrates the **platform's analytical and alerting capabilities**: data ingestion cadence, threshold logic, alert taxonomy, and reporting views. It intentionally uses simulated data so that every capability can be shown, including rare events (e.g., epidemic warnings) that a short field window may not produce.

It does **not** constitute field performance evidence for the HKT Rumen Bolus. Device-specific field performance (retention, long-term telemetry continuity, real-world alerting behavior) is established through structured farm pilots — see the companion document *Joint Field Pilot Study — Protocol*.
